# Blue/orange palette, deliberately not green/red

Every tuner ever made signals in-tune with green and out-of-tune with red, so a contributor finding
blue and orange here will reasonably assume it was an oversight and "fix" it. It was not an oversight.
Red and green is the single worst pairing for red-green colour vision deficiency, which affects roughly
8% of men — a population overlapping heavily with guitarists — and it also collapses in greyscale
screenshots and documentation.

Blue for flat, orange for sharp, bright neutral for in tune is distinguishable under every common
deficiency by construction, and legible against both light and dark terminal backgrounds.

## Consequences

Hue now encodes *direction* rather than *judgement*, which frees no channel for magnitude — so magnitude
must be carried by position on the coarse bar and by Strobe drift rate, never by colour alone. This is
the more actionable split anyway: hue says which way to turn, position and motion say how far.

Please do not "restore" green and red.
